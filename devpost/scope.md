# CHAOS KITCHEN — working title

A small, one-page dinner-rush game with ridiculous tickets and satisfying plate-building.

## Authorization Basis

The October 2, 2026 engineering handoff explicitly defines the concept, NOW/LATER boundary and directs immediate implementation of safe reversible choices. This document captures that authorization; it is not a claim of a separate document-review vote. Numerical tuning and fictional content are agent proposals within that delegation. Final hands-on acceptance remains open.

## The Unique Kernel

Read the chaos, make a plate, ring the bell: a funny customer reaction and a louder-looking kitchen reward each decision. The first 20–30 seconds must deliver a readable, funny, satisfying interaction.

October 3 founder-requested extension: optional Chef mode adds a timed brigade sequence: choose and prep ingredients → hot line → assemble plate → serve. Existing quick-play remains unchanged. This is a playable station prototype, not restaurant training. One active pan for this first slice; overlapping cooking and distinct illustrated cook characters remain later playtest-driven work.

## Who It's For

A casual phone or desktop player who wants a short, replayable restaurant-rush challenge rather than a management simulator. Entertainment is the user value; no health or business-performance claim.

## The Core Loop

Order arrives → read required ingredients and exclusions → choose ingredients → serve → reaction and score/combo → rising chaos → next ticket → shift receipt → replay.

## Inspiration & Identity

User direction: handwritten tickets, stainless-steel pass, marker notes, kitchen bell, steam, bold restaurant typography and tactile controls. Build like professionals, present like artists. Canonical AIZOYA logo and original Grace portrait remain unmodified; scene styling is game art, not a corporate rebrand. Working title is not cleared for external release.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

“FUN FIRST. MEMORABILITY FIRST. DEMO ENERGY FIRST.” Demonstrate AIZOYA's range and enjoy building.

## What "Working" Looks Like

A player starts, understands one ticket without a manual, serves a plate, sees feedback, encounters escalation, reaches a final score and restarts. Works by touch and keyboard; no hover dependence. App map and reproducible checks accompany it.

## The POC Boundary

One page, authored tickets, timer, eight ingredient buttons, serve, reactions, score/combo, escalating rounds, one panic action, replay, responsive layout, optional synthesized sound and polished finish. Local reliable gameplay; no runtime AI.

## Later

October 9 founder-authorized expansion supersedes the original eight-item/single-order boundary: expanded authored menu, method-specific cooking, optional multi-table service (Easy 1 / Medium 2 / Hard 3), announced Feeding Frenzy bonuses, cosmetic in-run rewards, original synthesized music choices, and an original Chef Franklyn / Chaos Star introduction. Preserve classic play. No actual inspector affiliation, film assets, real prizes, accounts, runtime AI, spend or production promotion. Franklyn's likeness is authorized but no verified reference photo is available in this recovered source; use a typographic inspector introduction until supplied.

More founder-authored stories and menu variations after feedback.

## Explicitly Cut

Accounts, authentication, multiplayer, leaderboards, persistent backend, monetization, native app, large database, cloud services, multi-agent runtime and infinite AI content. No recipe advice, allergy-safety certification or restaurant training.
